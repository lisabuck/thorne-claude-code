Our Systems

Refined over twenty years.

Behind every Thorne Group build is a set of systems we have refined over nearly 20 years. They support how your project is managed from start to finish, including a live online project schedule, structured budget tracking, clear communication throughout the build, and a dedicated aftercare programme that continues well beyond handover. It is this combination of experience and systems that helps projects run more smoothly, with fewer surprises along the way.

01 — Online System

From anywhere in the world.

We have built for numerous clients who travel extensively or are based remotely for the entire build. Our world-class systems and processes help keep everything on track regardless of where you are. One of the first residential builders in the region to introduce a live, cloud-based client portal, ours has been refined over the last decade to improve both the client experience and scheduling efficiency.

From anywhere in the world you can track your project, access documents, check the schedule, stay up to date on progress, and view photos as your home takes shape. Alongside the portal, Pete brought structure, governance, and financial oversight into how we run projects — strict cost approval protocols mean there are no surprises with changes to specifications or selections, trade discounts and supplier offerings are passed directly to you, and PC sum allowances are realistic. Efficiency protects your budget — it is not about cutting corners, it is about removing waste.

02 — Health & Safety

Everyone home to their whānau.

The safety and wellbeing of our staff, sub-contractors, clients and wider community is of the highest importance to how we operate. We genuinely care about every person who steps onto our sites, and we're committed to doing our best to ensure everyone gets home safely to their whānau.

This culture of safety consciousness is a responsibility at all levels — from the Board of Directors to tradespeople on site. We make no apologies for expecting high standards of safety practice and compliance, and encourage an attitude of "See Something, Say Something." All contractors are pre-qualified, and we engage an independent Health and Safety Advisor to oversee our practice and drive continuous improvement.

Safety technologies such as HazardCo and Site Safe help us track on-site activity and monitor and manage risks and hazards.

Our SiteWise Gold Accreditation status recognises our Health and Safety Policy and the exceptional quality health and safety systems we have in place.
