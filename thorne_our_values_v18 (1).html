01 — Family at the Heart

A family business — every build carries our name.

A culture built on genuine, personalised service and family values is at the heart of what we do. Our family name is on every project we deliver.

We run small teams led by foremen with at least twenty years of experience. That continuity is rare in this industry, and it is the reason our clients get consistent quality from project to project.

Most clients arrive through someone who has already built with us — a friend, a family member, a previous client. Several of our clients have built with us more than once.

That kind of loyalty is not bought with marketing. It is earned over years, one job at a time, by doing what we said we would do.

03 — Community & Sustainability

Commitment to community and sustainability.

As a family business we enthusiastically support community initiatives that align with our values and are relevant to our coastal location. Both the community work and the environmental work are part of how we operate, not a side activity.

Each year our team participates in a range of events and activities to promote health and wellbeing in our local community. Over the years we have contributed to Live for More (surf programme), Breast Cancer Support Services and Breast Cancer Foundation NZ, Women's Refuge, Variety Club, KidsCan, Hato Hone St John, Surf Lifesaving NZ — and more.

As a building company, we are very conscious of our environmental footprint. Our involvement in environmental projects in the Tauranga region is intended to benefit future generations. By planting native shrubs and trees in perpetuity at Summerhill Farm and Recreational Park — and other projects — we help mitigate the carbon footprint generated during construction.

As well as applying building methodologies that are sustainable and environmentally friendly, we work alongside architectural designers to create healthy homes using sustainable products, aiming for superior Homestar / Green Star ratings that reflect our commitment to energy efficiency and environmental responsibility.

Our commitment to ethical conduct, transparent operations, and respect for our community sets Thorne Group apart — and has earned us a reputation as a trusted leader in the construction industry.
