Pricing happens when there's enough detail.

Optimising your budget starts long before a contract is signed. It begins at design, runs through every system we have refined over twenty years, and continues with how we schedule, price and manage cost approvals. The savings come from removing waste — not cutting corners.

02 — Systems & Scheduling

Twenty years of refining how we build.

The savings do not stop at design. Time on site costs money — and every system we have built over twenty years exists to protect both.

Over two decades we have refined our systems at every stage of the process to optimise both labour and materials. We know where waste sits, how details can be executed better, and where supplier relationships can be sharpened.

It is not about cutting corners. It is about removing waste.

We were one of the first residential builders in the region to introduce a live online project schedule, and we have continued to refine it over the last decade. Time on site costs money — coordinated scheduling across our team, suppliers and subcontractors keeps the build moving, reduces downtime, and prevents the small delays that quietly add cost to a project.

03 — No Surprises

Accurate and transparent pricing.

An open, no-surprises approach. Comprehensive from the outset, locked in early, and never adjusted without your sign-off.

We take an open, no-surprises approach to pricing. Estimates are comprehensive from day one, costs are locked in as early as possible, and every change is signed off before it happens.

  • Comprehensive cost estimates from the outset.
  • Pricing locked in as early as possible to mitigate against price increases.
  • Trade discounts and supplier rates passed directly on to you.
  • PC sum allowances that are realistic, not optimistic.
  • Strict cost approval protocols so changes to specifications or selections never come as a surprise.
